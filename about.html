About Us

Philadelphia's trusted repair shop — fast, honest, and affordable since 2015.

Our Story

Center City Wireless was founded in 2015 by two lifelong friends, Marcus Chen and David Ortiz, who saw a problem in the repair industry: long wait times, overpriced service, and a lack of transparency. They set out to build a different kind of repair shop — one where customers are treated like family, diagnostics are always free, and every repair is backed by a real warranty.

What started as a tiny kiosk on Chestnut Street has grown into Philadelphia's go-to destination for device repair and accessories. We've fixed over 5,000 devices — from cracked iPhone screens to water-damaged MacBooks to PS5s with fried HDMI ports.

Today, our team of certified technicians continues that mission: to provide fast, reliable, affordable repairs using genuine parts — with a smile.

Our Mission

"We believe everyone deserves fast, honest, and affordable tech repair."

Technology connects us to the people and work that matter most. When a device breaks, it shouldn't cost a fortune or take weeks to fix. We're here to get you back up and running — same day, every time, with a warranty you can count on.
